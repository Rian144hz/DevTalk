# Code of conduct

Version 2026-10-14.

DevTalk is a place to practise English with other developers. Everyone here is learning, and making mistakes must feel safe.

## Do

- Be kind and patient. Most people here write and speak in a second language.
- When you correct someone, show the better version and explain it briefly. Corrections are edits, not grades.
- Keep to the topic of practice: work, code and the situations developers meet.
- Respect people's time: arrive on time for rooms and leave politely.

## Do not

- Harass, insult, threaten or follow someone who does not want to talk to you.
- Post hate or discrimination based on who someone is.
- Send sexual content or make sexual advances.
- Spam, advertise, run scams or ask for money.
- Pretend to be someone else.
- Share other people's private details (contacts, address, photos) without their permission.
- Record or transcribe other people's voices.
- Use the community if you are under 18.

## If something goes wrong

- **Block** a person to stop seeing each other.
- **Report** what happened. Reports go to the moderators, and the person is not told who reported them.

## What moderators can do

Moderators read reports and can send a warning, suspend an account for 1, 7 or 30 days, or suspend it for good. They act on what was reported and on the account's history.
