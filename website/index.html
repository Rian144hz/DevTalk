How it works

Ten minutes a day. Three small steps.

Practice is short on purpose. You write a few sentences, you see what to change, and the useful words come back later.

  1. 1

    Answer a situation

    Read a real work moment: a stand-up update, a code review comment, an interview question. Answer it in 3–5 sentences.

    Stand-up update · Yesterday you spent the day on a flaky test…
  2. 2

    Get it marked

    Your coach edits your text: words struck out and added, short notes in the margin, and a natural rewrite.

    We need to discuss about discuss it.
  3. 3

    Review the expressions

    Good expressions from the feedback go to your review queue and come back just before you forget them.

    roll out · phrasal verb · due tomorrow

Privacy

Your practice is yours.

Making mistakes should feel safe. So DevTalk keeps your writing close and tells you exactly where it goes.

  • Stays on your computer

    What you write stays on your computer, except the text you send to the coach you choose.

  • Encrypted API key

    Your API key is encrypted by your operating system's secure storage.

  • Voice is never recorded

    Voice goes directly between computers. Nobody records or transcribes it.

FAQ

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. DevTalk is free and open source (MIT licence). The coach uses your own AI key. Gemini has a free tier, and Ollama runs on your computer at no cost. Claude uses your own Anthropic API key.

Which AI does it use?

You choose: Google Gemini (free tier), Anthropic Claude, or a model in Ollama on your own computer. You can change it at any time in Settings.

Is my writing private?

Your practice stays on your computer. Only the answer you send for feedback goes to the coach you chose. With Ollama, nothing leaves your computer. Your API key is encrypted by your system.

Are voice rooms recorded?

No. Voice goes directly between the computers in the room. It is never recorded, never transcribed and never passes through our server.

Who can join the community?

Any developer who is 18 or older. The community is optional: you can use the coach and review without it.
